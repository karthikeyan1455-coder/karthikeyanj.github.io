Isolation and Purification of Plasmid DNA Containing hCG-β Subunit for Recombinant Protein Expression in E. coli

Academic project covering plasmid transformation, isolation, verification and purification, with results compared and recorded in a formal project report.

  • Transformed E. coli DH5α cells, screened positive clones on ampicillin plates (patch plating, quadrant streaking) and prepared glycerol stocks.
